How RidePals Works

Carpooling made simple, affordable, and eco-friendly

🚗
Step 1

Drivers post their trip

Heading somewhere? Post your trip in seconds — share your origin, destination, date, time, and how many seats you have open. Your listing goes live immediately for passengers to find.

🔍
Step 2

Passengers search for rides

Search by city or state to find drivers going your way. Browse available options, compare times, and pick the ride that works best for your schedule.

🤝
Step 3

Book a seat & coordinate

Found a match? Book your seat directly on the ride page. The driver's contact info is shared so you can sort out the details and hit the road together.

Why it matters

💰

Save Money

Split fuel and toll costs so everyone pays less for the same trip.

🌍

Cut Emissions

Fewer cars on the road means less carbon in the atmosphere.

⚡

Less Traffic

Every shared ride removes a car from the road for everyone.
