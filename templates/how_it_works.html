How RidePals Works

RidePals makes it easy for drivers and passengers to share rides, save money, and reduce their environmental impact.

1. Drivers share their upcoming trips

Drivers post details about their next trip — where they are leaving from, where they are going, when they plan to travel, and how many seats they have available. This creates a ride listing that passengers can discover.

2. Passengers search for matching rides

Passengers use the search page to look for rides that match their origin and destination. They can quickly browse available options, compare times, and find a driver heading in the same direction.

3. Carpooling made simple

Once a match is found, riders and passengers can coordinate the details of the trip. By sharing a ride, everyone helps reduce traffic, lower carbon emissions, and cut down on travel costs.

Why it matters

Every shared ride means fewer cars on the road, less fuel burned, and more money saved. RidePals is designed to make everyday carsharing and carpooling feel simple, safe, and convenient for both drivers and passengers.
